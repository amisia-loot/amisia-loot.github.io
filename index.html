Loot system

GP and fixed prices: base value × 2^((item level − ref.)/26) × slot weight × 2^(quality − 4). PR = EP / (GP + base GP). A raid in the addon keeps the system it started with.

How to export from your loot addon

Gargul

  1. Type /gl export, select the raid dates on the left (nothing selected exports everything).
  2. Click into the text box, Ctrl+A, Ctrl+C, paste it here.

RCLootCouncil

  1. Type /rc history, click Export and choose CSV or JSON.
  2. Ctrl+A, Ctrl+C, paste it here.

Amisia addon download

  1. Unpack it into the Interface\AddOns folder of your WoW Forever install. It records every raid on its own: who was in the raid group, who looted a tracked material (it learns the trade goods that drop in raids on its own; /amisia mats shows the list), every blue and epic item looted, what lay in the loot windows you open, and every item the master looter hands out (with MS, OS or SR from the roll round). Hand-outs appear in the award preview below like a Gargul export; loot without an award shows up as Other loot on the Nights tab and in the Loot Log. In game: /amisia roll [item] or Alt-click in the loot window starts a roll round, /amisia sr opens the soft-reserve list, /amisia award Name [item] records a hand-out by hand.
  2. After the raid type /amisia, press Export erstellen, then Ctrl+A, Ctrl+C and paste it here.
  3. Open the guild bank before exporting and leave it open for a few seconds (only when materials are tracked). The export then carries a count of every tracked material in the bank, which shows up on the Mats tab.
  4. The guild bank needs the officers set in the addon, the members' pledges and the guild bank log the addon read come along too: the Mats tab shows what is missing per material and the log under the requests.
  5. Boss kills in dungeons and raids: /amisia drops export (expert mode) shows the text Drops für die Website. Paste it here, press Preview import and Add the kills. They build the drop rates on the Loot Tables tab and carry no player names.
  6. Loot prio an officer changed in game (/amisia prio or the Prio button of the roll window) travels in the same export. Preview import lists it under Loot prio changed in game; Take them over puts it on the Loot Council tab, where the newest change of an item wins.

Both exports can go into the box at the same time. Then press Preview import, check the rows, pick a class for new raiders, and Import.

Every Gargul format works (TMB, JSON, custom, DFT, RRobin) and every RCLootCouncil export (CSV, TSV, JSON). Any other addon works too as long as each line carries the item link or id, the winner's name and a date. JSON exports also bring each winner's class. Entries that were already imported are recognised by their Gargul checksum, or by the same date, item and raider, and are skipped. Disenchanted items and items that are not in the Forever loot tables are ignored.

Guild & ledger settings
Backup

Copy the ledger as JSON to keep a backup, or paste a backup below to restore it.

Editors

People who may change the ledger. Enter their Discord username (as shown in their Discord profile, without the @) or their e-mail address. They sign in with Discord, or with an e-mail link if you entered an address.

Accounts that signed in

Link an account to its character to give it a rank: Officer, Raider or Trial. Linked members can set their own professions on the Roster tab.

Earlier versions

Every save keeps the version it replaced, the newest 40. Restoring writes a new version, so a restore can itself be undone.
