How to export from your loot addon

Gargul

  1. Type /gl export, select the raid dates on the left (nothing selected exports everything).
  2. Click into the text box, Ctrl+A, Ctrl+C, paste it here.

RCLootCouncil

  1. Type /rc history, click Export and choose CSV or JSON.
  2. Ctrl+A, Ctrl+C, paste it here.

Amisia addon download

  1. Unpack it into _anniversary_\Interface\AddOns. It records every raid on its own: who was in the raid group, who looted Mark of the Illidari, Heart of Darkness or one of the six epic raid gems, every blue and epic item looted, and what lay in the loot windows you open. Loot without a Gargul award shows up as Other loot on the Nights tab and in the Loot Log.
  2. After the raid type /amisia, press Export erstellen, then Ctrl+A, Ctrl+C and paste it here.
  3. Open the guild bank before exporting and leave it open for a few seconds. The export then carries a count of every tracked material in the bank, which shows up on the Mats tab.

Both exports can go into the box at the same time. Then press Preview import, check the rows, pick a class for new raiders, and Import.

Every Gargul format works (TMB, JSON, custom, DFT, RRobin) and every RCLootCouncil export (CSV, TSV, JSON). Any other addon works too as long as each line carries the item link or id, the winner's name and a date. JSON exports also bring each winner's class. Entries that were already imported are recognised by their Gargul checksum, or by the same date, item and raider, and are skipped. Disenchanted items and drops that are not from one of the nine Burning Crusade raids are ignored.
